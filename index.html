Hi, I'm Weiqi Zhang.

I study how to learn useful representations from medical images and make better use of limited data.

I'm a Ph.D. student in the Graduate School of Informatics at Chiba University. My research interests include medical image analysis, dataset distillation, and self-supervised learning.

Previously, I received my M.Sc. in Information Science from Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST), where I worked on X-ray image analysis and bone mineral density estimation.
